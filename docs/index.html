Discovery Dojo

The multiplayer sales room, built on DeepSpace

Paste a prospect's website. AI researches the company, plays a buyer who pushes back and coaches every reply, while a manager sits in, recommends the next move or steps in to close. Scored on MEDDIC, ranked on a live leaderboard.

01 Research

A buyer from a real company

Firecrawl reads the site, Exa finds web and news results, and Claude builds a buyer with hidden pains, objections and a MEDDIC ground truth.

02 Talk

Rep-led, streamed live

The rep opens with one of three AI openers. Each buyer reply types out on every screen at once, through one shared record.

03 Coach

A coach after every reply

Buyer signal, deal temperature, MEDDIC coverage, talk ratio and three next moves. Built from the transcript only, so it cannot leak the answers.

04 Manage

The manager decides

Recommend a move and it lights up for the rep. Pin a note on a moment. Or step into the call and speak to the buyer as the rep's boss.

05 Close

Deals you can actually win

Earn it and the buyer books the next meeting, agrees a pilot or buys. Share a one-pager and the buyer reads it.

06 Review

Scored, with evidence

Six MEDDIC scores with quotes, pains found and missed, turnarounds and the deal outcome. Every scored call lands on the leaderboard.

For DeepSpace developers

Patterns worth copying.

Stream through a record

Create an empty turn, then tools.update it with the growing text every 250 ms. RecordRoom fans it out to every viewer over the socket they already have.

Secrets in a collection

A field on a shared row reaches every browser. A collection with read: false for members never does. Reveal it through an action that checks the role.

Actions are the gate

Action tools run with record permissions off, so every action reloads the session and checks the caller before acting.

Facts over presence

Presence is self-reported and can drop. Roles come from the session row; presence only carries ephemeral state like typing.

Numbers in code

The model returns sub-scores; code clamps, totals and computes talk ratio. A tidy() pass cleans every stored model string.

Degrade, never die

Every research source is optional, and every model call walks a fallback chain on 402, 429 or 5xx.

Trade-offs, honestly.

Main trade-off

Records over HTTP streams

About four writes a second during a reply, in exchange for one channel that reaches the rep and every manager with no extra plumbing.

Left out

Voice and video, for now

Every coach and scoring feature works on the transcript. useVoiceAgent and LiveKit rooms are the next step, writing into the same records.

Known limits

Said out loud

Research runs in one request (a background job is the fix), one room per app, any member can join a call as manager, PDFs are not read by the buyer.
